HOW IT WORKS

From a local voice
to a real-world pilot.

Jan Samadhan Setu creates a simple chain: citizens report → AI organises the issue → the right stakeholders collaborate → pilots are tracked publicly.

Jharkhand State-wide

Expand across all districts, onboard universities and open CSR adoption lanes with district-level dashboards.

Pan-India Replication

Extend partnerships beyond Jharkhand with shared taxonomies and a national network of universities, industry and local bodies.
