A simple digital platform where people can report local problems, the system can sort them by urgency, and universities, startups and industry partners can help develop solutions.

Citizen reports a problem with description, urgency and location.

2. CONNECT

Reports are sorted and matched with suitable solution partners.

Solutions are tested, tracked and their impact is recorded.
